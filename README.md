## 📊 Featured Case Studies & Projects

### 1. Credit Risk Auditing & Machine Learning | Undergraduate Thesis
**Tubungan Teachers' Multi-Purpose Cooperative (TTMPC)**  
*Stack: Python, Pandas, Scikit-Learn, PostgreSQL, Supabase, FastAPI* · [View Repository](https://github.com/Gtabs2004/TTMPC-THESIS)

#### The Problem
The cooperative relied on manual, paper-based ledgers where borrower payment histories were fragmented, making it difficult for management to assess true loan delinquency or identify risky borrowers.

#### What I Found (Data Auditing)
* Audited 7 years of historical records (932 loans, 237 members) and corrected tracking errors across 432 historical loan accounts, revised the recorded loan delinquency rate from a distorted **85.85% down to an actual 38.7%**.
* Found that **70% of "settled" loans** were paid off by immediately taking out a larger loan on the same day, it hides rising debt in 60% of repeat borrowers.

#### What I Built (Analytics & Decision Support)
* Engineered 21 pre-loan application variables to train a Predictive Risk Model in Python (`HistGradientBoosting`, ROC-AUC: 0.659).
* Built a dashboard screen for loan officers that explains an applicant's risk score, showing which specific factors (such as savings or repayment history) raised or lowered their risk.
* Cleaned and structured the historical datasets Loan Demand Forecasting & Risk Model
  

### **2. Strategic Loan Portfolio for Financial Cooperatives**
* **Domain:** Prescriptive Analytics & Portfolio Optimization · [View Repository](https://github.com/EJTeope/Credit-Allocation-for-Financial-Cooperatives)
* **The Problem:** Determine how a financial cooperative should allocate a modeled ₱5,000,000 lending budget across micro-enterprise, agricultural, and personal loans without exceeding a 3.5% default loss threshold.
* **The Finding:** Personal loans offered the highest interest rate (16%), but their 9% default risk ate up the entire safety buffer which makes it economically unviable.
* **The Solution:** Formulated a constrained Linear Programming model in Python (PuLP), deriving an optimal allocation (66.7% Agricultural Credit, 33.3% Micro-enterprise) that maximized annual portfolio revenue at **₱483,333** while maintaining 100% compliance with institutional risk limits.

### **4. SDG 13: The Climate Change & Health Risk Paradox Dashboard**
* **Domain:** Econometric Modeling & Interactive BI · [View Repository](https://github.com/EJTeope/SDG13-Climate-Health-Risk-Dashboard)
* **The Problem:** Explore how rising global temperatures affect public health vulnerability across 150+ countries.
